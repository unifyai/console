The City of London office market has demonstrated resilience through Q1 2026. Prime equivalent yields stand at 5.15%, broadly stable over the last two quarters.

5 Special Assumptions

  1. The property is assumed to be free from structural defects and environmental contamination.
  2. All floor areas have been taken from the client's briefing and have not been independently measured.
  3. The break clause at year 5 is a tenant-only break, exercisable on 6 months' written notice, subject to no outstanding rent arrears.
  4. No allowance has been made for any rights, easements or encumbrances not apparent from the title documents.
  5. This valuation does not account for any potential dilapidation liabilities at lease expiry.
